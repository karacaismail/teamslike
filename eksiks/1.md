# Google Meet Deep Feature Inventory (2026) — Beyond the Baseline

**Competitive intelligence inventory for an SMB-focused video conferencing challenger, as of June 2026. Sources are overwhelmingly official Google (support.google.com, workspaceupdates.googleblog.com, developers.google.com, workspace.google.com), supplemented by Pexip, Figma, Recall.ai, and tech press (9to5Google, Android Authority, Android Central) for newly announced items.** Note: my web-search and subagent budgets were exhausted before I could fully verify two thin areas (native remote-control / picture-in-picture, and the precise edition matrix for "Studio look/sound"); those are flagged as lower-confidence in the Caveats.

## TL;DR

- **Google Meet's 2026 differentiation is the layers around the call, not the call itself:** the Gemini AI layer (Ask Gemini, real-time speech-to-speech voice translation, "Take Notes for Me" now extended to in-person/Zoom/Teams meetings), a maturing developer surface (REST API, Media API for real-time bots, Add-ons SDK, Workspace Events Pub/Sub), and deep Workspace-native admin/compliance plumbing (BigQuery export, Vault retention, automated SEC/FINRA compliance recording). An SMB challenger can't match this breadth dollar-for-dollar and should instead compete on simplicity, flat pricing, and the granular UX gaps below.
- **Moderation, hardware interop, and telephony are quietly broad and mostly gated behind paid tiers:** a true waiting-room queue (distinct from the legacy Quick Access knock model), Audio/Video/Chat/Present locks, Teams/Zoom/Webex interop from Meet rooms (Webex/Zoom free & built-in; Teams via paid Pexip Connect), occupancy/people-count sensors with a Room Insights dashboard, and Meet Global Dialing across 100+ outbound countries.
- **Most exploitable SMB gaps:** high-value features are fragmented across editions and frequently "rolling out" rather than uniformly GA; native screen annotation and whiteboarding depend on third-party add-ons (FigJam, Miro, Lucidspark) since Jamboard's sunset; and **native real-time remote-control of a shared screen is effectively absent.** A challenger that ships clean, included-by-default equivalents — without edition gating — has a clear wedge.

For every feature I capture (a) Google's name, (b) what it does, (c) editions where specified, (d) availability/status, (e) category.

## 1. Host & Moderation Granularity

All Host Management safety features require the meeting-level **Host Management** toggle (available to all Workspace editions + Individual; admins set per-OU defaults). Status GA unless noted.

- **Waiting rooms (new experience)** — True holding queue; admit one-by-one or "Bring everyone in," send participants back to the queue, send one-way chat to those waiting (they can't reply); Calendar "Bring in automatically" option. Rollout completed on/before **Nov 21, 2025**. Distinct from Quick Access. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Quick access** — Legacy toggle; OFF forces knock-to-join, gates dial-out and host-joins-first. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Meeting access tiers: Open / Trusted / Restricted** — Restricted (Calendar-invited only; all others knock) is **not available to consumer accounts.** *(Host/Moderation)*
- **"Anyone can ask to join, including people who dial in" checkbox** — Unchecking disables knocking and **auto-denies anonymous users / third-party note-taker bots** — a direct anti-bot control. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Audio lock** — Blocks self-unmute; unsupported mobile clients are removed & prompted to update. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Video lock** — Locks all cameras off (same mobile removal behavior). *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Chat lock** — Disables chat for non-hosts. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Present lock** — Only host/co-hosts can present. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Mute everyone / Mute all** — One-click; re-unmutable unless Audio lock is on. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **End meeting for everyone** — Ends call + closes breakout rooms. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Lock propagation to breakout rooms** — Main-room locks carry into newly launched breakout rooms. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Co-hosts (up to 25)** — Business Standard/Plus, Essentials, Enterprise (Starter/Essentials/Standard/Plus), all Education, Workspace Individual; must be in-meeting to appoint; can't appoint from breakout rooms. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Grant host controls (in-meeting promotion)** — Promote a participant to co-host mid-meeting; required to let an external user record. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **"Host must join before anyone else"** — Disappears for users who have the new waiting-room feature. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **View-only / "Everyone is a viewer" roles** — Viewers get no camera/mic/present. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Send to waiting room (eject-to-queue)** — Move a disruptive participant back to the queue rather than removing. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Remove participant / Mute individual** — Per-participant from People panel. *(Host/Moderation)*
- **Require explicit participant consent before notes/recording/transcription (admin)** — New admin control; rolling out from **May 5, 2026**, tied to editions including notes/recordings/transcripts. *(Host/Moderation/Privacy)*
- **Continuous meeting chat in Google Chat** — In-meeting messages persist in a linked Google Chat conversation; externals see chat only while present (with "external" banners); respects Chat retention + DLP; host can disable per meeting. Rapid Release **Dec 8, 2025**, Scheduled **Mar 23, 2026**; Business Starter/Standard/Plus, Enterprise Starter/Standard/Plus, Frontline Starter/Standard/Plus. *(Host/Moderation/Collaboration)*

## 2. In-Meeting Tools (Layout, Presenting, Whiteboarding)

- **Dynamic layouts (Auto)** — AI grid with **Portrait tiles** (crops background to fit more faces); revertible to Tiled (legacy)/Sidebar/Spotlight. All editions + personal. Rapid Release **Mar 31, 2025**, Scheduled **Apr 17, 2025**. *(Layout)*
- **Dynamic tiles** — Splits up to **3 in-room people** into individual tiles with AI active-speaker detection. **Requires Meet hardware + license.** *(Layout)*
- **Face match** — Companion-mode users label their tile with their name; face *detection* only, not recognition; requires hardware. *(Layout)*
- **Multi-pin (up to 6)** — Pin limit raised from 3 to 6. *(Layout)*
- **Spotlight** — Lock a speaker/presentation full-screen for yourself. *(Layout)*
- **"Show my full video to others"** — Forces uncropped self-tile; recommended for sign-language interpreters. *(Layout/Accessibility)*
- **Flexible self-view / Color-sampled tile theming** — Persisting self-view prefs; camera-off tiles themed from profile-image color. *(Layout)*
- **Automatic framing** — Keeps web users in frame (continuous recenter when a virtual background is on). *(AI Video)*
- **Native annotations on shared content** — Pen, disappearing ink, text, stickers, shapes over shared content; **co-annotators** from web/mobile/touch hardware. *(In-Meeting)*
- **Whiteboarding via add-ons (FigJam / Lucidspark / Miro)** — Launched from the Activities panel on web and Board 65 / Desk 27; **no first-party Google whiteboard** since Jamboard's sunset. *(Integrations)*
- **Present options: A tab / A window / Your entire screen** — Tab share is privacy-optimized and best for video/motion. *(Presenting)*
- **Share device audio when presenting** — Share window/full-screen device audio (beyond tab audio); announced **Dec 19, 2025** recap. *(Presenting)*
- **Share stereo sound** — Stereo audio sharing for music/video; **Dec 2025**. *(Presenting)*
- **Control Google Slides within Meet** — Content/controls/audience in one view. *(Presenting)*
- **HDMI content sharing cross-platform** — Present into Webex from Meet hardware and from Cisco into Meet via HDMI; enabled with Zoom interop. *(Interop)*
- **Logitech Scribe whiteboard camera** — Brings physical whiteboard content to remote participants with auto contrast enhancement. *(Hardware)*
- **Companion mode** — Second-device join for chat/polls/Q&A/share/captions without an audio feed. *(In-Meeting)*
- **Raise hand / palm-to-camera gesture** — Gesture alternative to the button. *(Accessibility)*
- **Notable gaps:** no native real-time "give remote control" of a shared screen; no first-party annotation engine independent of add-ons.

## 3. Gemini AI — Lesser-Known Capabilities

- **Ask Gemini in Meet** — Private in-meeting assistant: summarize, "catch up on what you missed" (pre-join catch-up needs Take Notes for Me on), "what did someone just say," key takeaways/decisions/action items; private per user; references captions + permitted Workspace files + web; no caption persistence; not in breakout rooms; auto-starts at ≥2 participants; dial-in users hear consent announcement. **Editions:** Sept 2025 Business Plus first → Enterprise Standard/Plus, Business Standard, Business Plus. Rollout **Sept 29, 2025**; Business Standard **Jan 26, 2026**; +7 languages (FR/DE/IT/JA/KO/PT/ES) **Feb 2, 2026**; mobile **Feb 9, 2026** (one language at a time). *(Gemini)*
- **Ask Gemini relocated prompt box (web)** — Persistent bottom-left box; rollout from **May 26, 2026**. *(Gemini/UX)*
- **"Take Notes for Me"** — Google Doc with summary, action items, new **Decisions** section; follow-up email adds "suggested next steps." *(Gemini)*
- **Customizable notes sections** — Toggle Summary/Decisions/Next steps/Details + detail level from in-call menu; rolling out **Apr 30, 2026**. *(Gemini)*
- **Notes recipient controls** — Web hosts pick recipients (all guests / in-org guests / hosts only; default in-org). *(Gemini/Host)*
- **Take Notes for Me for in-person & Zoom/Teams meetings** — Cloud Next 2026 (Apr 22, 2026); cited **110M+ attendees** in the prior month (8.5x YoY). *(Gemini)*
- **Localized auto-artifacts** — Recordings/transcripts/notes auto-generate in host's preferred language (FR/DE/IT/JA/KO/PT/ES). *(Gemini)*
- **Studio lighting (low-light AI)** — Expanded to web **and** mobile mid-2025. *(Appearance)*
- **AI Makeup / "touch up appearance"** — Movement-adaptive AI makeup, stackable. *(Appearance)*
- **Cloud accelerator for visual effects** — Curated backgrounds processed in cloud for low-spec devices; Business Standard/Plus, Enterprise Standard/Plus, Workspace Individual in eligible regions; not on VDI. *(Appearance)*
- **Real-time speech translation (AI voice dubbing)** — Near-real-time translated synthetic voice preserving tone/pitch; one language pair/meeting; "Speech translation" badge; "Translate me" consent. Beta I/O **May 20, 2025** (EN↔ES, AI Pro/Ultra); **GA for select Workspace plans Jan 27, 2026** (admin control, ON by default, disable per OU); pairs EN ↔ ES/FR/DE/PT/IT; **mobile from April 2026**; room hardware hears but doesn't translate its own speech. *(Gemini/Accessibility)*
- **Gemini settings (admin)** — Single Admin console surface governing Ask Gemini, speech translation, smart features at domain/OU/group; mostly default ON. *(Gemini/Admin)*

## 7. Telephony / PSTN

Billing accounts must be registered in supported countries (US, CA, UK, FR, ES, PT, DK, SE, CH, IE, NL, DE, IT, AT, BE…). Turkey/Cyprus dial-in limited to Enterprise editions. *(Telephony)*

## 8. Developer / Extensibility

- **Meet Media API (Developer Preview)** — Real-time WebRTC access to live audio/video + control streams (no chat/transcript stream) for AI agents/bots — native, no extra-participant bot required; in preview all participants must be in the preview program. ## Recommendations

1. **Lead with "no edition roulette."** Google's biggest SMB-facing weakness is fragmentation — waiting rooms, Ask Gemini, speech translation, 25 co-hosts, compliance recording all live on *different* tiers and are often still "rolling out." Ship the moderation + AI-notes core **included in every paid plan** and message it as such. **Threshold to revisit:** if Google folds Ask Gemini + speech translation into Business Starter at no uplift, this advantage erodes.
2. **Close the two native gaps Google left open:** (a) first-party **real-time remote screen control** and (b) a **native annotation/whiteboard** that doesn't require a third-party add-on or a Series One device. These are concrete, demoable wins versus Meet today.
3. **Match the AI-notes table stakes early, then differentiate on privacy clarity.** "Take Notes for Me" (now even for in-person/Zoom/Teams) and Ask Gemini are becoming buyer-expected. Offer equivalent summary/action-item capture, and turn Google's own friction (consent banners, admin-required consent, edition gating) into a "consent-clear, on by default, no add-on" message.
4. **Don't try to out-build the admin/compliance moat; out-simplify it.** WORM compliance recording, Vault, BigQuery export, and per-OU policy are enterprise-grade but heavy. For SMBs, offer a 3-click retention + simple audit export rather than a BigQuery pipeline. **Threshold:** only invest in WORM/eDiscovery parity if you target regulated SMBs (finance/healthcare).
5. **Treat telephony and Teams interop as paid-partner parity, not core.** Google charges for Global Dialing and Teams-via-Pexip; bundling reliable dial-in/out and Teams-room join into your base price is a clean differentiator for mixed-stack SMBs.
6. **Expose a real-time media/event API from day one.** Google's REST API is post-meeting-only and its real-time Media API is still Developer Preview with onerous prerequisites — the reason a whole bot-vendor ecosystem (Recall.ai et al.) exists. A clean real-time transcript/event webhook API is a developer-adoption wedge.

## Caveats

- **Search/subagent budget exhausted mid-research.** Two areas are lower-confidence and should be re-verified against support.google.com before sales use: (a) **native remote-control / picture-in-picture / minimized floating window** — third-party guides indicate native remote control is absent and annotation requires add-ons, but I could not pull a definitive first-party page; (b) the **exact edition matrix and GA-vs-rolling-out status for "Studio look," "Studio sound," and Imagen "generative backgrounds"** — confirmed Studio lighting (web+mobile) and AI Makeup exist, but the full Studio suite edition gating is partially inferred.
- **"Rolling out" ≠ universally available.** Many 2026 items (consent-before-recording, notes customization, high-res video, adaptive auto-redirect, relocated Ask Gemini box) were still in gradual rollout at the time of research; treat dates as rollout *start*, not completion.
- **Caption language counts vary by source.** "80+" translated-caption languages and "60+" come from third-party trackers and Google copy at different dates; verify the current number on the live captions help page.
- **Speech-translation language pairs are expanding fast** (EN↔ES/FR/DE/PT/IT at last check) and quality is explicitly "improving continuously" per Google — re-check before competitive claims.
- **Third-party sources** (Pexip, Figma, Recall.ai, Gecko/Damson partner blogs, Interprefy, Maestra) were used for interop, add-on, telephony, and translation color; cross-check any single-sourced figure (e.g., per-minute Global Dialing rates, exact bot-API behaviors) against primary Google docs before publishing externally.
- **No primary verification of "Studio sound" as a distinct shipping feature name** in the sources retrieved; it may be marketed under "adaptive audio" / "noise cancellation" rather than a standalone "Studio sound" toggle.
